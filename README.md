☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis — Interactive Excel Dashboard
An end-to-end Excel data analysis project exploring six months of coffee shop transactions. The goal is to turn raw point-of-sale data into clear business insights about revenue, product performance, store performance, and peak sales hours.
Tools: Microsoft Excel · Power Query · PivotTables · PivotCharts · Slicers
Dataset period: January 1 – June 30, 2023
Records analyzed: 149,116 transactions

📌 Business Problem
A coffee shop business needs to understand what drives sales, which product categories contribute most to revenue, how its locations compare, and when customer demand is strongest. This project organizes transaction-level data into an interactive Excel dashboard to support those decisions.
🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze total sales revenue and quantity sold.
- Compare sales across product categories and individual products.
- Evaluate performance across store locations.
- Identify sales patterns by month, weekday, and hour.
- Build an interactive dashboard that helps users explore the data without manually filtering raw records.
🧰 Tools & Techniques
- Microsoft Excel: data analysis, formulas, and dashboard presentation
- Power Query: data preparation and transformation
- PivotTables: summarize sales by category, location, date, and time
- PivotCharts: communicate trends and comparisons visually
- Slicers / filters: make dashboard exploration interactive

🔍 Findings from the Dataset
1. Product category performance
- Coffee generated the highest revenue at $269,952.45.
- Tea followed at $196,405.95.
- Coffee and tea together contributed approximately 66.8% of total revenue.
- Bakery contributed $82,315.64, while Drinking Chocolate contributed $72,416.00.
2. Store performance
- Hell's Kitchen: $236,511.17
- Astoria: $232,243.91
- Lower Manhattan: $230,057.25
Revenue was relatively close across the three locations. Further analysis of footfall, staffing, operating hours, and product mix could help explain the differences.
3. Sales by hour
The highest-revenue hours in the dataset were:
- 10:00: $88,673.39
- 09:00: $85,169.53
- 08:00: $82,699.87
This suggests that morning hours are an important sales period and may deserve attention when planning staffing and product availability.
4. Monthly trend
- June recorded the highest monthly revenue at $166,485.88.
- May followed at $156,727.76.
The dashboard can be used to explore the monthly trend and investigate possible drivers such as product mix and transaction volume.
💡 Business Recommendations
1. Protect coffee and tea availability: These categories contribute the largest share of revenue, so monitor stock levels and availability during peak periods.
2. Plan for morning demand: Review staffing, preparation, and queue times around 8:00–10:00.
3. Compare store-level product mix: Revenue is similar across locations; category and product comparisons may reveal opportunities to tailor inventory.
4. Investigate monthly growth: Compare June with earlier months and validate whether the increase is driven by transaction count, quantity, or average transaction value.
5. Track performance consistently: Use the dashboard's filters to monitor revenue, quantity, and product/category performance over time.
